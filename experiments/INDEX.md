# Experiments

|---|---|---|---|---|
| [EXP-01](EXP-01_CorrectedEIVRerun_2026-11-12/README.md) | 2026-11-12 | Does the paper's 4.67× IQ-motion "bias factor" survive the corrected EIV method (Rasad `rasad-eiv/2`)? | Complete | No. The corrected fit reproduces the slope, but plain 1/σy² WLS reproduces it too; correcting for measurement error alone gives a slope 0.94× OLS. The ratio is a site-weighting effect that rests on an untested σy assumption. Recommendation: withdraw or reframe. |
| [EXP-02](EXP-02_EIVMethodPanel_2026-11-12/README.md) | 2026-11-12 | Do the established EIV methods agree with the updated PCR on ABIDE? | Complete | They split by the σy assumption, not by method. With constant σy all 12 estimators fall between −0.00125 and −0.00134; with the paper's site σy, every method that uses it gives a 3.4–5.1× flatter slope. Updated PCR = York; PCR-2 = structural ML. |
| [EXP-03](EXP-03_VariancePartitionControls_2026-11-12/README.md) | 2026-11-12 | How much FD variance is noise, and what is the IQ slope under controls? | Complete | A G study is not identifiable (one scan each). Image metrics bound FD estimation noise at ≤34% (raw) or ≤46% (log) of within-site variance. With site, age, sex and diagnosis controlled the slope shrinks 40–44% and stays negative. IQ reliability lower bound 0.64 vs assumed 0.945, which would steepen the slope. |
| [EXP-04](EXP-04_FrameLevelGStudy_2026-11-12/README.md) | 2026-11-12 | G study from frame-level FD: how much motion variance is noise, and what is the slope with measured noise? | Complete | 853/935 subjects. Run-mean FD is 84–95% reliable within a scan; noise is 5–16% of within-site variance, about a tenth of the paper's σy. PCR-2 with measured noise equals OLS on the log scale. Between-run ICC about 0.4 (one site). |
| [EXP-05](EXP-05_RobustnessChecks_2026-11-12/README.md) | 2026-11-12 | Do the ABIDE-I results survive reviewer-style robustness checks? | Complete | Mostly. With few-cluster inference the controlled IQ slope is borderline (log p 0.04–0.05, raw 0.07). Noise estimate, QC inclusion and single sites do not matter. PCR-2 is unbiased on the log scale, 12% low on the raw scale under proportional noise. |
| [EXP-06](EXP-06_Abide2Replication_2026-11-12/README.md) | 2026-11-12 | Do the results replicate in ABIDE-II under a plan frozen in advance? | Complete | Measurement results replicate (G = 0.88; scatter-free York fails, χ² 13.4). The controlled IQ-motion slope does not (−0.0018, p = 0.68; pooled p = 0.072). Diagnosis effect on motion replicates strongly. IQ × diagnosis reverses sign. n=460, 11 sites, partial public release. |
